♟ Brand Story · Est. 2012 · Plant City, FL

14 Years. of clubs & Tournaments. One Brand.

It started with a chess club, 50 enthusiastic kids, and a teacher with a vision. It is still all about promoting chess.

It is all about promoting chess.

That has been true from the very first shirt — and it remains true today. These are Ms. Rose's own words, and they have guided everything we have done since the beginning.

"As a teacher in Florida, I led a chess club for over seven years with over 50 enthusiastic kids. The team shirts you see here were my creation, and they have been a big hit among our members. Encouraged by their popularity, we decided to make them available to others."

That decision — to open up what was working inside one classroom to the wider chess community — is how Wit-T-Shirt was born. Not from a business plan. From a chess club, a teacher, and 50 kids who loved the game.

↑ Back to Top
♟ ♟ ♟

From that starting point, the mission grew. In Ms. Rose's words:

"Since that time we've gone beyond — designing for other teams, schools, districts, and chess organizations to promote our Wit-T design chess shirts. We've done the work, getting in the door to events, proving our worth at all levels, and giving back to the chess community."

That is the thread that runs through all fourteen years. Not just making shirts — earning a place at every level of the chess world, from school hallways to state tournaments to online storefronts, and always putting the community first.

The collection expanded alongside the players. More designs, more styles, more sizes — because the chess community wearing our shirts was growing too, and every one of them deserved something worth wearing.
